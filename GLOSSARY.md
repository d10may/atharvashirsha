# Atharvashirsha Recitation Walkthrough

A digital recitation companion and reading guide for the sacred Sanskrit hymn Śrī Gaṇapati Atharvaśīrṣa, designed for solo and group chanting without external audio playback.

## Structural Concepts

**Hymn**:
The complete sacred Sanskrit text composed of traditional thematic sections.
_Avoid_: Song, prayer, chant-track, audio

**Verse**:
A numbered traditional thematic section (khanda) of the hymn, demarcated by double dandas.
_Avoid_: Stanza, paragraph, chapter

**Phrase**:
A single metrical clause or breath unit within a verse, terminated by a single danda.
_Avoid_: Sentence, line, segment

**Word**:
An individual Devanagari or transliterated orthographic unit within a phrase.
_Avoid_: Token, substring, character-group

**Danda**:
A traditional Sanskrit punctuation mark indicating a phrase breath pause (`।`) or verse termination (`॥`).
_Avoid_: Pipe, vertical bar, slash, stop

## Prosody & Chanting

**Mātrā**:
The fundamental unit of Sanskrit poetic meter, measuring the prosodic time duration of a syllable.
_Avoid_: Beat, tempo, interval

**Laghu**:
A short, light syllable in Sanskrit prosody carrying the weight of one mātrā.
_Avoid_: Short syllable, quick beat

**Guru**:
A heavy, long syllable in Sanskrit prosody carrying the weight of two mātrās.
_Avoid_: Long syllable, double beat

**Avartan**:
A complete, uninterrupted recitation of the entire hymn from the opening invocation to the final verse, repeated in traditional practice.
_Avoid_: Loop, rerun, cycle, repetition

## Presentation & Modes

**Walkthrough Mode**:
The active recitation view where text progresses with word-level or phrase-level visual guidance.
_Avoid_: Player mode, karaoke view, tracking view

**Continuous Read Mode**:
A distraction-free, full-text reading view displaying the hymn without visual stepping or highlighting.
_Avoid_: Book mode, static mode, reader mode

**TV Mode**:
A presentation view with viewport-scaled typography, auto-hiding controls, and full keyboard navigation for projectors or large displays.
_Avoid_: Fullscreen mode, presentation mode, projector view

**Script**:
The orthographic writing system used to render the hymn, selectable as Devanagari, Roman (IAST), or Bilingual.
_Avoid_: Font, language, alphabet
